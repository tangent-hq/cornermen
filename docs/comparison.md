## Cost and access

- **Cornermen:** a free template. You pay for your Hermes model connection and hosting, if needed. ChatGPT/Codex subscription login is supported; API billing is another option.
- **Grok Bot:** advertised plans are $30, $100 and $200/month, with included usage and additional usage charges. It can already be included in an eligible plan.
- **OpenClaw:** also supports multiple providers and ChatGPT/Codex subscription login. Its token spend depends on the model and workload; no measured cost advantage over it is claimed here.
- **Muse:** a free usage tier with paid upgrades. Meta announced a US rollout; its help pages still describe limited regional availability.
- **ChatGPT Dots:** included with eligible Pro and workspace plans. Official documentation excludes EEA, UK and Switzerland from the personal Pro rollout; eligible business and enterprise access differs.

The reason I use Cornermen is the combination: separate specialists, a workspace I can work in directly, model choice and accumulated context I control. The template packages the roles and coordination around that. It still takes setup; it is not a hosted service you can use without configuring anything.
